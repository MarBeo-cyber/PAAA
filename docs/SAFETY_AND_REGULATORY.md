# Safety and Regulatory Notes

PAAA is a research prototype and must not be marketed as:

- diagnostic device;
- treatment system;
- clinical screening tool;
- Parkinson detection tool;
- substitute for medical evaluation.

Allowed positioning:
- longitudinal observation;
- personal baseline tracking;
- biofeedback;
- self-regulation;
- data collection for human review.

PAAA may suggest consulting a professional when persistent deviations are detected.

It must not provide diagnoses.
