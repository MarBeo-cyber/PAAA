# Scientific References — PAAA

## Personal Autopoietic Adaptive Agent

PAAA is positioned as a longitudinal neurofunctional observation and adaptive self-regulation platform.  
It is **not** a diagnostic or therapeutic medical device.

---

## 1. Autopoiesis and Embodied Cognition

**Maturana, H., & Varela, F. (1980)**  
*Autopoiesis and Cognition: The Realization of the Living*  
→ Operational closure, self-maintenance, adaptive continuity, structural coupling  
→ Applied in PAAA: neurofunctional continuity, adaptive self-observation

**Varela, F., Thompson, E., & Rosch, E. (1991)**  
*The Embodied Mind*  
→ Cognition as embodied action, sensorimotor coupling  
→ Applied in PAAA: embodied sensing, situated baseline

**Clark, A., & Chalmers, D. (1998)**  
*The Extended Mind. Analysis, 58(1), 7–19*  
→ Cognition extends beyond the brain; external cognitive scaffolding  
→ Applied in PAAA: wearable as cognitive extension

---

## 2. Neurofunctional Biomarkers

**Postuma, R. B. et al. (2012)**  
*Identifying prodromal Parkinson's disease: pre-motor disorders in Parkinson's disease*  
*Nature Reviews Neurology*  
→ Voice, gait, tremor as early biomarkers years before clinical diagnosis  
→ Applied in PAAA: priority monitoring domains

**Little, M. A. et al. (2009)**  
*Suitability of dysphonia measurements for telemonitoring of Parkinson's disease*  
*IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering*  
→ Voice biomarkers (jitter, shimmer, HNR) for longitudinal monitoring  
→ Applied in PAAA: voice feature extraction module

**Arora, S. et al. (2015)**  
*Detecting and monitoring the symptoms of Parkinson's disease using smartphones*  
*Methods, 81, 41–46*  
→ Smartphone-based tremor and gait monitoring  
→ Applied in PAAA: passive sensing layer

---

## 3. HRV and Autonomic Monitoring

**Task Force of the European Society of Cardiology (1996)**  
*Heart rate variability: standards of measurement, physiological interpretation, and clinical use*  
*Circulation, 93(5), 1043–1065*  
→ HRV as autonomic nervous system indicator  
→ Applied in PAAA: physiological continuity layer

## 4. Personal Baseline and Anomaly Detection

**Goldberger, A. L. et al. (2000)**  
*PhysioBank, PhysioToolkit, and PhysioNet*  
*Circulation, 101(23)*  
→ Longitudinal physiological baseline methodology  
→ Applied in PAAA: individual z-score baseline engine

**Münch, M. et al. (2012)**  
*The role of daylight for humans: gaps in current knowledge*  
*Nature and Science of Sleep, 4, 67–77*  
→ Circadian and seasonal factors in physiological parameters  
→ Applied in PAAA: contextual baseline correction

## 5. Safety and Regulatory Framework

**EU Medical Device Regulation (EU) 2017/745 (MDR)**  
→ Classification of software as medical device; PAAA is positioned below diagnostic threshold

**FDA Digital Health Center of Excellence**  
*Policy for Device Software Functions (2022)*  
→ Guidance on non-diagnostic wellness monitoring software

**ISO 13485:2016** — Medical devices quality management  
→ Reference for future clinical validation pathway

## 6. Privacy and Data Protection

**GDPR — Regulation (EU) 2016/679**  
→ Applicable to health-adjacent personal data; informs PAAA privacy by design

**HIPAA — Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (US)**  
→ Reference framework for health data handling in US deployment context
