# references.md — PAAA
## Personal Autopoietic Adaptive Agent

### Scientific and Philosophical References

This document collects the theoretical and scientific foundations that inspired the design of PAAA.

PAAA is positioned as a longitudinal neurofunctional observation and adaptive self-regulation platform.  
It is not a diagnostic or therapeutic medical device.

---

# 1. Autopoiesis and Embodied Cognition

**Autopoiesis and Cognition: The Realization of the Living**

Relevant concepts:
- operational closure
- self-maintenance
- adaptive continuity
- structural coupling

Application to PAAA:
- neurofunctional continuity
- adaptive self-observation
- preservation of functional coherence

# 3. Design Principles

PAAA does not search for disease.

PAAA searches for persistent deviations from individual neurofunctional continuity.

Allowed output:

“Persistent variation detected relative to historical baseline.”

Not allowed:

“Possible neurological disease.”
